# SoundFx App Icon Design

## Brand Identity
SoundFx is a volume-normalization utility that makes every song and video play at the same comfortable loudness — it tames loud tracks and lifts quiet ones. The brand personality: calm, precise, trustworthy, engineering-grade. The icon must read instantly at 16px and feel at home on a Chrome toolbar, an iPhone home screen, and an Android launcher.

## Symbol Design
A single centered motif on a rounded-square app-icon tile: five vertical sound-wave bars whose heights transition from chaotic to equalized. The three left bars have uneven heights (tall #D55E00 vermillion, short #E69F00 orange, tall #56B4E9 sky blue) and the two right bars settle to one identical calm height (#009E73 bluish green) — visually narrating "uneven loudness becomes even". Bars have generous rounded caps (fully rounded ends, stadium shape), equal width, evenly spaced on a strict grid. One intentional asymmetry: a thin horizontal "target level" line in white passes behind the bars at the height where the right bars settle, hinting at the normalization target. No neural-network nodes, no swoosh, no hexagons, no gradients.

## Typography
None. No letters, no text, no monogram — pure symbol only.

## Color Scheme
Okabe-Ito colorblind-safe palette ONLY, flat solid fills with zero gradients: background tile solid pure white #FFFFFF with a very subtle 1/48-width #0072B2 border on the tile edge; bars in #D55E00 (vermillion), #E69F00 (orange), #56B4E9 (sky blue), and #009E73 (bluish green); target line deep blue #0072B2 at 60% visual weight (thin, 1/24 of tile width). Flat vector look, crisp edges, no shadows, no bevels, no glassmorphism, no 3D, no noise texture.

## Scalability
Composition uses a 12-unit grid: tile corner radius ~22% of width, bars occupy the central 60% horizontally and 55% vertically, equal margins. Shapes bold enough to stay legible at 16×16 px; no strokes thinner than 1/24 of the tile width.

## Technical Specifications
1024×1024 square PNG, flat vector-style raster, centered composition, solid fills, sRGB. The rounded-square tile fills ~92% of the canvas on a pure white outer background. lighting: none (flat design, uniform illumination, no light direction, no color temperature variation). style lock: flat minimal vector; mood lock: calm precise; palette lock: Okabe-Ito hexes above.
