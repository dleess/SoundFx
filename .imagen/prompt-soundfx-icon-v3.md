# SoundFx App Icon Design

## Brand Identity
SoundFx is a volume-normalization utility that makes every song and video play at the same comfortable loudness — it tames loud tracks and lifts quiet ones. The brand personality: calm, precise, trustworthy, engineering-grade. The icon must read instantly at 16px and feel at home on a Chrome toolbar, an iPhone home screen, and an Android launcher.

## Symbol Design
A single continuous audio waveform line drawn horizontally across the center of a rounded-square app-icon tile: on the left half the waveform oscillates wildly with large uneven peaks (stroke transitions through #D55E00 vermillion into #E69F00 orange), then in the right half it smooths into a calm, even, small-amplitude steady wave (#56B4E9 sky blue settling into #009E73 bluish green) — visually narrating "uneven loudness becomes even". The line is one bold continuous stroke with round caps and joins, stroke width 1/14 of the tile width. Two thin horizontal white guide lines above and below the right-side calm wave mark the normalized band. No neural-network nodes, no swoosh, no hexagons, no gradients.

## Typography
None. No letters, no text, no monogram — pure symbol only.

## Color Scheme
Okabe-Ito colorblind-safe palette ONLY, flat solid fills with zero gradients: background tile solid deep blue #0072B2; bars in #D55E00 (vermillion), #E69F00 (orange), #56B4E9 (sky blue), and #009E73 (bluish green); target line pure white #FFFFFF at 60% visual weight (thin, 1/24 of tile width). Flat vector look, crisp edges, no shadows, no bevels, no glassmorphism, no 3D, no noise texture.

## Scalability
Composition uses a 12-unit grid: tile corner radius ~22% of width, bars occupy the central 60% horizontally and 55% vertically, equal margins. Shapes bold enough to stay legible at 16×16 px; no strokes thinner than 1/24 of the tile width.

## Technical Specifications
1024×1024 square PNG, flat vector-style raster, centered composition, solid fills, sRGB. The rounded-square tile fills ~92% of the canvas on a pure white outer background. lighting: none (flat design, uniform illumination, no light direction, no color temperature variation). style lock: flat minimal vector; mood lock: calm precise; palette lock: Okabe-Ito hexes above.
