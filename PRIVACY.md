# SoundFx Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-09_

SoundFx does not collect, transmit, or sell any user data.

## What the extension does

SoundFx processes audio locally in your browser to normalize loudness. All audio processing happens on your device; no audio, browsing activity, or personal information ever leaves your browser.

## What is stored

Your settings (on/off state, target loudness, compressor and EQ parameters, per-site presets) are saved with Chrome's `storage.sync` API. This data stays within your browser profile and, if you are signed into Chrome, is synced by Google across your own devices. The developer has no access to it.

## What is NOT collected

- No personal information
- No browsing history
- No audio content
- No analytics or telemetry
- No third-party services
